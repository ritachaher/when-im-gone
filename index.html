When someone dies, the hardest part after the grief is not knowing where anything is.

Most families spend weeks piecing together paperwork. It doesn't have to be that way.

"Where's the will?"

Your family shouldn't have to hunt for a solicitor's name during the worst week of their lives.

"Which accounts exist?"

Bank accounts, pensions, insurance policies. Most families miss at least one. Some never find them.

"What did they want?"

Burial or cremation? Which music? Who to call first? Don't leave them guessing when it matters most.

A note from the founder

I built this because my own family didn't know where to start.

When my grandad died, we spent weeks in his study with a cardboard box and a notebook, piecing together accounts, contacts and his wishes, most of which we only half-knew. The grief was hard enough. The hunting was cruel.

When I'm Gone is the small, simple thing I wish he'd had time to do for us. It isn't a will, and it isn't clever. It's just a way to write things down before you don't have time to.

If you have any questions, you can email me directly: info@whenimgone.life. A real person will reply, usually the same day.

A. Dinatale

Common questions

Honest answers to the things people ask most

Is this a will? +
No. A will is a legal document about who inherits what. When I'm Gone is the practical companion to a will. It tells your family where to find the will, who your solicitor is, which accounts you have, who to call first, and what you wanted at your funeral.
Do I have to share passwords or login details? +
No. We deliberately don't ask for passwords or login credentials. Your family doesn't need to log into your accounts. They need to know which accounts exist, who to contact, and where to find the paperwork. That's what we capture.
What if I forget my password? +
When you set up, you receive a one-time recovery code. Keep it with your will or with someone you trust. If you forget your password, the code lets you back in. We can't reset it for you (that's the point of the encryption), but the recovery code always will.
Can my family read it without me being there? +
Yes. That's the whole purpose. They use the recovery card you've left them to unlock a clear, calm, read-only view of everything you've written. No tech skills needed.
What happens to my journal if your company closes? +
Your journal works without us. Because everything is encrypted on your device, it doesn't depend on our servers being online. You can also export your journal as a PDF at any time - so you'll always have a copy you can hold in your hand.
Is my information really private? +
Yes. Your journal is encrypted on your device with your password before anything ever leaves it. We can't read it. Our staff can't read it. If we were ever compelled to hand over data, all anyone would see is encrypted gibberish.
Is it really a one-off payment? +
Yes. £12.95 once. No subscription, no renewals, no upsells. Free updates for life. If we ever change pricing, anyone who has already paid keeps their account at no extra cost.
Can I see what it looks like before I pay? +
Yes. There are sample journals on this page so you can see how it looks and what kind of questions it asks. £12.95 buys you the full thing once you're ready.
