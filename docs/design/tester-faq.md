# When I'm gone — questions you might have

Short honest answers to the things testers tend to ask. Link this from your invite, or paste individual answers into emails as they come up.

---

## Can two of us use it on the same computer?

Yes, but today you each need your own **browser profile**. A browser profile is a separate account inside the browser — your own bookmarks, logins, and storage, kept apart from anyone else's on the machine.

**In Chrome or Edge:**
1. Click your name or picture in the very top-right corner of the browser
2. Choose "Add profile" (or "Add a new profile")
3. Pick a name and picture for your profile
4. Open the app link from the new profile window — it'll be a fresh install for you, with no connection to the other person's journal

**In Safari on a Mac:** File → New Profile.

**On iPhone or iPad:** Safari doesn't have profiles, so two people can't have separate journals on the same iPhone yet. If that's you, use each person's own phone — it's only a few minutes per session.

If two people you know are both testing and share a computer, let me know — I'm planning to build proper multi-journal support in a later version, and hearing this is a real need would help me prioritise it.

---

## What happens if I forget my password?

The **recovery code** you print at the end of setup is the second way in. Type it on the unlock screen and you're back in — no need to remember the password.

Keep the printed sheet somewhere your trusted person can find it (a sealed envelope, a safe, a folder labelled "If something happens to me"). That's the point — it's not meant to stay with you; it's meant to be findable later.

---

## What if I lose BOTH the password and the recovery code?

Honest answer: nobody can recover it. Not even me.

This is the price of real privacy. If I could reset your account, so could anyone who impersonated you. The app is built so that if both keys are lost, the journal goes with them. If that scares you, print a second copy of the recovery code and give it to someone you trust — that's exactly what it's for.

---

## Can you (the developer) see what I write?

No. The technology makes it impossible, not just a policy promise.

Everything you type is locked on your device using a key made from your password. I don't have a copy of that key, and I can't make one. If a court order arrived tomorrow asking for your journal, I could only hand over scrambled text that nobody can read without your password or recovery code — not even me.

---

## Does it work without internet?

Yes. Everything works offline — setup, writing, reading, unlocking. The only thing that needs the internet is the optional "Save a copy to the cloud" button, which sends a locked-up version of your journal to a backup server (as unreadable scrambled text).

You can use the app on a plane, a bus, in a coffee shop with dodgy wi-fi — all fine.

---

## What happens if you disappear?

"When I'm gone" is designed to keep working even if I stop maintaining it. Everything lives on your device. If my website vanishes, the version of the app already installed on your phone or computer keeps running, and the data stays yours.

If you've backed up to the cloud, that's hosted by a different company (Google Firebase) and doesn't depend on me being around.

---

## What's the backup button actually saving?

The same scrambled, unreadable version of your journal that's on your device. It's uploaded to a small, private space only you can write to. Nobody — not me, not the backup company — can read the contents without your password or recovery code.

If you lose your device, you can install the app fresh on a new one and pull the backup down.

---

## Can I try it without entering real information?

Absolutely — and honestly, that's what I'd recommend for a first look. Use made-up names, fake addresses, whatever. The point of the test is to see if the app makes sense, not to collect real data. When you're ready to use it for real, either keep going with what you've entered or wipe it and start over (there's a "Wipe everything" button in the sidebar).
